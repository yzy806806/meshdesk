How phone clients connect
  1. Phone connects to the shared/entry node on the proxy port (default 52888)
  2. Reality TLS handshake authenticates and encrypts the connection
  3. SOCKS5 protocol runs over the encrypted smux session
  4. Traffic is forwarded through the mesh to an exit node, then to the internet
